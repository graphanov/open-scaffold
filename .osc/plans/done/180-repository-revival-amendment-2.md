# Amendment 2: 180-repository-revival

## Parent

180-repository-revival

## Date

2026-10-06

## Learning

GitHub's changed-plan validation caught a shared lifecycle defect: close helpers moved plans into done/ while retaining active/backlog Status text. The same defect left 51 older completed records with a folder/status mismatch. The previous corpus snapshot preserved those lint outcomes and therefore did not prevent the new instance.

## New direction

Keep the maintenance goal and authority boundary. Make CLI and supported shell closure align the genuine Status with done while preserving prose, fenced examples, goal, criteria, amendments, and line endings; repeated closure repairs stale Status without another mission stamp. Validate the actual packed newcomer plan after closing. Reconcile the older done-folder Status mismatches using that same idempotent helper; do not claim new delivery or alter historical acceptance results.

## Impact on acceptance criteria

AC1, AC3, and AC6 gain explicit closure/folder-Status verification as a concrete instance of coherent instructions, dependable newcomer lifecycle, and repository record reconciliation. AC2, AC4, AC5, AC7, and AC8 retain their original wording and scope. Source/published version separation and owner merge/publication gates remain unchanged.
