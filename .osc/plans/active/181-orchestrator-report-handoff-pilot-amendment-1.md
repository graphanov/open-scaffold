Primary outcome remains binary factual recovery of 12 preregistered facts, with wrong task/run/head attribution incorrect. Report all three matched pairs and descriptive paired differences; no statistical significance, model superiority, hundred-agent or cost claim. Before launch pin source/formatter/driver/instruction versions and explicit unknown inherited model routing. The proposed envelope is 15 native invocations, at most 180 seconds per invocation, 2700 seconds aggregate wall proposal, 65536 complete-input UTF-8 bytes, no transport or score-driven retries. The external controller monitors/cancels native tasks; it does not claim an OS kill guarantee or provider spending/token cap. A failed or timed-out attempt remains an outcome.
